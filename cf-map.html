CF Map

One grammar, many research branches.

The map does not say CF solved these fields. It says what changes about the question when retained consequence and reachability are made explicit.

consequential transition retained consequence constraint reachability

Branches

Memory

Memory becomes durable consequence that changes future reachability, not just retrieval of stored content.

Learning

Prior experience alters organization so the same future input may no longer produce the same response.

Intelligence

Ask when retention, constraint, self-reconstruction, adaptation, and anticipation become organized enough to model futures.

Autonomy

Ask who is paying for this constraint now, and whether an organization helps preserve the conditions of its continuation.

Science

Inspect inherited nouns, measurements, definitions, approximations, and representation debt before solving downstream.

Communication

Recorded knowledge is accumulated formation. Historical influence is not truth, but provenance must track both.

Energy

Represent energy as part of what computation is physically reachable, not merely as an external budget.

Physical systems

Wear, drift, reference fabrics, terrain, channels, and material constraints become history-bearing observables.

Robotics and body

Body may be whatever physical resources are incorporated into actionable closure, not necessarily one human-sized chassis.

Waste

Waste can be reframed as currently unreachable material while preserving thermodynamic and economic honesty.

Observer envelope

Human survivability has been an epistemic constraint. Changing the observer changes reachable observation.

History

History matters when prior events remain instantiated in present structure, state, constraints, relations, or recovery routes.

Map discipline

Every branch should keep four layers separate: plain-language intuition, technical formulation, current status, and how we would falsify it.
