Authority Compiler

A query-indexed, evidence-bound, fail-closed reference compiler

  • PUBLIC / VERIFIED REFERENCE FRAGMENT
  • Finite implemented fragment
  • No new physics claim

Claim boundary: Authority Compiler is a deterministic finite reference implementation. It is not a new quantum theory, not a new quantum-information theorem, not a consciousness system, not a truth machine, not an AGI safety proof, and not a universal authority engine.

What it is

Authority Compiler checks whether a declared representation or evidence object is sufficiently qualified for a declared query, carrier, intervention semantics, and authority channel. It emits typed judgments with certificates and fail-closed HOLD states instead of turning authority into a global score.

Why it exists

The underlying problem is simple and sharp: the same projected value does not automatically imply the same provenance, the same formation, or the same lawful future use. A system that deduplicates only by surface content can erase the very distinction a later governance question needs.

What did not survive

The audit removed the larger theory claims: no new quantum-information theorem, no new quantum dynamics, no new physics, no global authority scalar, no global status lattice, no universal negative-certificate theorem, no free-monoidal minimality theorem, no universal lineage/contextuality section obstruction, and no claim that vectors or embeddings inherently cannot retain provenance.

Research lineage

The compiler emerged from Marr/Thiel source materials, Hunter's later rearticulation discussion, two QAR rounds, independent specialist rotation, Grok Lane A formalization, Specialist 09 falsification, BigQuery comparator work, a TPR supplement, and Codex implementation. Model outputs were research instruments and adversaries, not institutional endorsements or a substitute for human peer review.

The defensible result is not that AI proved a theory. It is that a broad adversarial machine audit killed most novelty and theory claims, retained a narrow operational structure, and that surviving structure became a deterministic, fail-closed finite reference compiler.
