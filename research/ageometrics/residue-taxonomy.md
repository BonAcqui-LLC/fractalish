# Residue Taxonomy

Proposed residue classes:

## Temporal residue

Sequence, duration, timing, irreversibility, and path information lost when only terminal or compressed geometry is retained.

## Causal residue

Mechanistic and intervention-response information not recoverable from observational geometry alone.

## Provenance residue

Origin, authority, chain of custody, evidentiary status, manipulation history, and source integrity.

## Contextual residue

Information that depends on the operational or measurement context rather than a context-free state assignment.

## Algorithmic residue

Differences in generative process, program length, or computational depth not visible in the resulting form.

## Semantic residue

Meaning, obligation, intent, normativity, and reference not guaranteed by adjacency or similarity alone.

## Identity residue

Continuity, lineage, copying, restoration, replacement, and checkpoint history.

## Energetic residue

Stored stress, work, depletion, metabolic burden, compute cost, maintenance debt, and hidden operational risk.

## Claim boundary

These classes are proposed working categories, not a settled ontology.

Not yet established:

- that the classes are exhaustive
- that the classes are mutually independent
- that the same partition will survive cross-domain benchmarking unchanged
