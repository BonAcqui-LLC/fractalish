## 2. Claim-by-claim reconciliation

### 2.1 Claim 2 (attributed to Event 002): "Compositional Superposition — composing two constraints yields linear summation." — SYSTEM_AUDITOR verdict: RETRACTED.

**Actual Event 002 claim (verbatim):**
- Disposition: `COMPOSITION_REQUIRES_DECOMPOSITION`. "Composition as a monolithic cross-domain role does not survive this hunt."
- §1.3 finding 2: "**Genuine additive systems exist and are common** … they represent a large class where composition adds no information beyond the components." Additivity is treated as the **anti-case (A01–A04)**, not as a claim.
- §1.3 finding 1: most biological "composition" effects are state-mediated sequencing, "not a distinct structural role."
- §1.4: "The idea that composition is always consequential dies (anti-cases are common)."

**Reconciliation:** Event 002 explicitly argued the **opposite** of "compositional superposition." The audited claim exists nowhere in the Event 002 receipt. The RETRACTION therefore applied to a claim that was never made. **Verification: FAIL (strawman retraction).**

### 2.2 Claim 3 (attributed to Event 003): "History Compression Theorem — any history can be compressed into a 3-parameter vector." — SYSTEM_AUDITOR verdict: RETRACTED.

**Actual Event 003 claim (verbatim):**
- Disposition: `STATE_CLOSURE_IS_TASK_DEPENDENT`. "A state that closes for prediction may fail for intervention."
- §1.3 finding 3: "**Genuine non-Markovian dynamics exist and resist finite-dimensional closure.** Power-law viscoelastic materials exhibit relaxation that cannot be exactly represented by any finite set of internal variables. This is not a failure of imagination—it is a mathematical impossibility for certain relaxation spectra."
- Final disposition (§27): "The search for a single 'minimal sufficient state' for a system is **abandoned**."

**Reconciliation:** Event 003 explicitly claimed the **failure** of finite-state compression (S06, A07 anti-cases). The "History Compression Theorem" is a direct inversion of the receipt's central finding. **Verification: FAIL (strawman retraction).**

### 2.3 Claim 1 (attributed to Event 001): "Generator Invariance — the relational signature is identical whether human-made or natural." — SYSTEM_AUDITOR verdict: DOWNGRADED.

**Actual Event 001 claim (verbatim):**
- Disposition: `TARGET_REQUIRES_DECOMPOSITION`. "GENERATOR as a monolithic cross-domain role does not survive this hunt."
- §1.2 finding 1: the generative package decomposes into specification/biasing/coupling.
- §1.2 finding 5: "Three anti-cases resist the frame entirely" (phenomenology, legal reasoning, measurement inference).

**Reconciliation:** No "generator invariance" across provenance classes is claimed in Event 001; the receipt's headline is that the generator does **not** survive as a unity. **Verification: FAIL (fabricated claim, then downgraded).**

### 2.4 Claim 4 (attributed to Event 004): "Cross-Scale Grammatical Invariance." — SYSTEM_AUDITOR verdict: DOWNGRADED to HELD with narrowing (self-similar/geometry-constrained networks only).

**Actual Event 004 claim (verbatim):**
- Disposition: `DOMAIN_SCALE_DEPENDENCE_REQUIRES_TASK_OBSERVER_INDEXING`.
- §1.2 finding 2: "**A small class of relations survives scale/domain translation with role intact.** Conservation relations (mass, energy, charge) are the clearest examples." — i.e., invariance is claimed **only** for constraint-level relations (conservation, causality, equilibrium), explicitly **not** for mechanism-level roles (findings 1, 8).
- Final disposition (§29): mechanism-level roles transform; "constraint-level relations are more stable … than mechanism-level relations."

**Reconciliation:** The audited label "Cross-Scale Grammatical Invariance" overstates and reverses the receipt's claim: invariance was never asserted for the grammar's operators/roles, only for constraint-level physical invariants, and the disposition explicitly requires task/observer indexing. **Verification: PARTIAL — label misstates claim; narrowing direction is not a correction of the source but an artifact of the misstatement.**

### 2.5 Claim 5 (attributed to Event 005): "Non-commutative Recovery — sequence of interventions always yields asymmetry." — SYSTEM_AUDITOR verdict: UPHELD WITH NARROWING.

**Actual Event 005 claim (verbatim):**
- Disposition: `RETAINED_CONSEQUENCE_REQUIRES_DISTRIBUTED_RELATIONAL_FORM`.
- §1.2 finding 3: "[SCENT-REMOVE] [SCENT-RESTORE] are the strongest tests."
- §1.2 finding 6: "Genuinely requires extended history" for some systems.
- Event 002's actual composition finding: order-irreducible composition "exists but is rarer than assumed."

**Reconciliation:** Event 005 does not claim "sequence always yields asymmetry"; Event 002 explicitly confines order-irreducibility to a minority of cases. The universal quantification ("always") is not in the receipts. The UPHELD direction happens to coincide with a plausible narrow claim, but the audit again audited a stronger-than-made claim. **Verification: PARTIAL — direction fair, scope fabricated.**
