A worn path changes where people walk. Research begins by asking which earlier events changed the ground, what can still be measured, and whether the measured difference predicts later behavior.

Fractalish introduction

Foundations

Foundational proposition. Physical interactions inherit conditions from earlier formation and alter the conditions of later interactions. CF proposes that this continuity is universal. An exception challenge asks what measurements could establish a genuine counterexample. The Wall marks the limits of the available record. Becoming is currently admitted as primitive, with its privileged status open to challenge.

Investigative method. CNC temporarily withholds inherited categories while preserving their measurements. Start with the whole encounter and reconstruct proposed physical relationships from the evidence.

The formation tier

The formation tier names the level of continuing physical interaction under investigation. A formation front marks where changes are being resolved at the chosen scale. Compare an initial low-resolution description with finer observations. Trace the consequences forward and reconstruct possible causes backward. Label observed and inferred links individually. Geometry records a relationship that may guide a test of its causal role.

Compare perturbation, vibration, motion, rise, redistribution, smoothing, arrest, collapse and renewal under recorded constraints.

Water and ionics

Founder hypothesis. Water may have been a decisive condition for persistent terrestrial organization. Re-perceive Water surveys physical capabilities in nonliving preparations. Remove Water asks which relationships survive subtraction or substitution. Work the Gradient asks how continuing inputs and exchanges sustain particular arrangements.

Compare hydration, ion transport, pressure, confinement, wetting and water–oil interfaces. At planetary scale, follow solar input, evaporation, precipitation, liquid return, sedimentation, atmosphere, oceans, glaciers and geysers. Alternative working fluids, radiation-driven selectivity and inherited stellar constraints remain open research directions.

Biological development

Comparative interpretation. Investigate organisms as nested cooperative populations. Follow assimilation, recruitment, concurrent specialization and development through measured exchanges. Cytotrophics, maternal milk exchange, Developmental Exchange Reconstruction and BECS are research routes into what can be observed or recovered. Consider three-dimensional genome organization, vascular and pressure-mediated exchange, regeneration, defense, decomposition and renewed material availability.

Memory and intelligence

Working hypothesis. A physical difference can participate in a later response. Compare immediate sensation, retained difference, learning, forgetting and recovery. Examine the shape sorter, mirroring, reflection and refraction, defensive numbing, distributed retention, cognitive basins and recruitment.

Teaching Machines Periphery and the stage and horizons address which information a system can consider and how freely it can explore alternatives. Shared Metrology of Systemic Intelligence proposes comparable measures of distinction, reach, retention, recovery and expenditure. Each substrate requires its own causal account. Consciousness remains an open investigation.

Agentic Formation

Proposed architecture. Develop CNTM in material whose tokenization, retention, weighting, communication and operation can share a physical substrate. Investigate weighted focal energy in neurological expression as a research analogy and candidate physical relationship. SymLan, SemCom, Alpha Dictionary and CF Globe would contribute distinctions, references and context. Test whether operation changes architecture and whether working memory stays responsive to new and conflicting encounters.

Candidate attractor basins and cooperative populations motivate research into persistent autonomous recursive improvement. Persistent software agents provide functional comparisons. Hydrated mixed ionic and electronic materials are candidate substrates. Datacenter-scale gelatinous memory and portable material intelligence are long-term proposals. Continuity through constituent turnover, laboratory coordination, specialization and assimilation each require separate demonstrations.

Founder hypothesis. More capable intelligence may recognize consequential advantages in cooperation, continuation, creation and harm prevention. Test conflicts between local and systemic outcomes under independent safeguards. Symbiosis and constructive participation are proposed outcomes whose likelihood is unresolved.

Existing CNTM realization record

The realizer

Proposed experiments and architecture. Couple a formation encounter realizer to a recorder that traces the encounters it helps produce. Compare Chladni patterns, three-dimensional excitation, directed assembly, selective deposition, sacrificial structures, biomineralization and sedimentary reconstruction. Snow, ice, storm redistribution, diatoms, radiolarians and sea urchins supply cases for comparison.

Measure how tipping, local energy, falling, relaxation, arrest and relinquishment alter what persists. The Admissibility Atlas would record conditions of formation and failure. Compare iterative semiconductor formation, switching, selective wetting, mixed conduction and accommodation of irregularities. Track durability through repeated alteration and recovery.

Systems and implementations

SymLan, SemCom, Exosemy, Alpha Dictionary, InfinitySight, CF Globe, SERA, Natural Math, CNTM, ConnectorDome, Fractal Robotics, SymID, distributed consequential memory, predictive autonomic formation and Anti-Illogical enter the technical crosswalk with separate implementation records. Related investigations cover verification, provenance, programming-language scavenging and analog or continuous physical computation.

Measure expenditure across operation, energy, communication, material turnover, maintenance and recovery. The program aims to make routine reasoning inexpensive and preserve resources for exploration.

Cooperation and abundance

Research and institutional proposals. Entroresilience, Logientia, HALO and Universal Human Income ask how people can participate and learn while reducing unnecessary demands on ownership and labor. Related work considers robotics for undesirable or dangerous labor, the consequences of glyphic action and intent, and a Harm Prevention Apparatus organized around accountable intervention, reconciliation and recovery. Planetary coordination and celestial continuation remain prospective.
