Working register | October 28, 2026

Measure the formation before naming the mechanism.

Every proposal declares its materials, frame, native quantities, perturbations, controls, uncertainty, falsifiers, provenance, and reset conditions.

PROPOSED / NOT RUN

Priority 1

EXP-OF-001 — Pulsed quasi-2D copper electrodeposition

Question: Does a bounded first growth history alter later branching response under a matched second pulse after controlling for deposited charge, visible geometry, electrolyte state, temperature, and cell geometry?

System and frame

Transparent quasi-2D cell, dilute copper sulfate, copper electrodes, current-limited low-voltage supply, fixed overhead camera, scale target, temperature and conductivity logging. Declare pixel size, frame rate, electrical sample rate, and clock uncertainty before the run.

Native quantities

Voltage, current, charge, temperature, conductivity, branch-tip velocity, occupied area, branch count, skeleton length, local width, and transition time, each with units.

Perturbation and probe

Equal-charge first histories with different duty cycles, followed by the same second probe. Preserve raw video and sensor streams from baseline through relaxation.

Controls

Fresh-cell and sham-wait controls, randomized order, equal charge, fixed gaps, blind image labels, declared cleaning/replacement, Faradaic accounting, and native electrochemical models.

Falsifying result: No reproducible matched-probe difference after controls; the effect disappears under blinded replication; or an ordinary electrochemical state model predicts the response without an additional history variable.

Safety: Low voltage does not eliminate chemical or short-circuit risk. Follow the copper-sulfate safety data sheet and local hazardous-waste rules; do not discharge solution to drains. This register does not itself authorize a run.

Priority 2

EXP-OF-002 — Viscoelastic accommodation and rupture

Use standardized silicone or gelatin specimens, load cell, displacement sensor, environmental logging, and camera. Compare subcritical cyclic histories under one matched probe, then separately study rupture and post-rupture response.

Falsifier: A preregistered viscoelastic/fatigue model predicts held-out responses within error and an extra history variable provides no reproducible gain.

Priority 3 / HOLD

EXP-OF-003 — Conducting-polymer or memristive history response

Compare equal-energy histories with order changed, then apply an unseen probe family. Compete against resistor/RC, lookup-table, adaptive-filter, and native hysteresis models.

HOLD: Hardware, fabrication route, and safety/reproducibility contract require creator selection before implementation.

What success would and would not mean

A useful result would localize a reproducible retained physical difference that changes a later matched response and beats native baselines. It would not validate CF universally, demonstrate intelligence, prove that branching is special, or make “fractal” morphology an explanation.
