Matter matters. Formation first. Measurement governs.

Fractalish investigates how physical configurations arise, persist, interact, reorganize, fail, and alter what becomes physically accessible next.

WORKING / NON-CANONICAL / NOT A THEORY FREEZE

This page does not replace CF v0.4 RC1, the October concordance authorities, or any frozen project artifact. It is a public research synchronization connecting those records to the later October physical-formation work.

Formation, measurement, and representation are not the same thing.

Formation

The ongoing physical configuration and interaction under investigation.

Measurement

A physical interaction with that formation, made through a declared instrument, resolution, frame, and uncertainty.

Representation

A state vector, diagram, equation, event chain, label, or software record built from selected measurements for a declared question.

Conventional representations remain indispensable instruments. The question is what they preserve, what they omit, and whether an omitted difference changes a later measured response.

Ongoing formation exceeds a simple event chain.

The historical grammar event → change → retention → response remains useful for logging and intervention design. It is not assumed to be the complete physical structure. Interaction, retained organization, current response, and modification of future accessibility can overlap and reciprocally alter one another.

A snapshot is not automatically a state. A state is not automatically the physical configuration. An arrow is not automatically an independent cause.

Accommodation, instability, and rupture belong inside the evidence.

Accommodation is measured as the perturbation envelope within which a declared relationship remains bounded. The research record must include approach-to-transition signatures, the transition itself, cascading effects, failed runs, recovery, and the altered response afterward.

Mobility + constraint

Measure reachable continuations, thresholds, transport, relaxation, and retained topology together. No material phase is presumed universally necessary.

Nested formation

Test local and global coupling with localized interventions and common-driver controls.

Rupture

Do not discard the run when identity, continuity, or return to baseline fails. The post-transition formation is part of the result.

Physical comparison does not require a symbolic comparator.

A configuration can respond differently because coupling, thresholds, geometry, chemistry, or prior modification differ. “Comparison” becomes a stronger causal claim only when a separately testable comparator office survives subtraction. Universal comparison remains a research proposition, not a law.

CF Evolution follows capability, not a predetermined life boundary.

The working questions concern persistence, reproduction or reconstruction, differential continuation, inheritance, cooperative formation, and the acquisition of additional response modes. Biology supplies rich cases; it does not dictate a universal substrate script.

A key distinction is between preserving structure, altering later response, acquiring an additional response mode, and changing the ability to develop further capacities. Each requires a different measurement contract.

Engineered synapses and CNC must earn their categories.

The engineering program tests history-dependent physical response, morphology-dependent access, continued organization, nested coupling, rupture/recovery, and capability acquisition. It keeps PERCEPT, ATAL, RIGOR, R2R, ASF, Natural Math, and the Authority Compiler as instruments and governance machinery.

Operational continuity is not subjective consciousness. This program does not claim that embodiment is uniquely necessary for intelligence or that CNC has demonstrated an engineered synapse.

Alpha Dictionary remains a proposal.

The supplied working direction treats glyphs as active containers with deep measurement archives, contextual activation, unresolved relational debt, and revision through later consequence. The existing 7D container design and frozen Symlan and Natural Math authorities remain intact. The controlling Alpha Dictionary / Exosemy source packet was not located in this audit, so no replacement architecture is introduced.
