Roadmap | Updated June 2026

Built, underway, and not built yet.

Some parts of Fractalish already exist in code, documents, and device evidence. Some are active engineering targets. Some are still future work.

Established

  • Fractalish public site, field guides, documents, study guides, and collaboration hub.
  • Public Fractalish repo.
  • Public Cognitive Basin platform repo.
  • Cognitive Basin / BasinLab technical release and implementation disclosure.
  • Natural Math field guide, current papers, and implementation references.
  • CNTM realization materials.
  • Motorola activation evidence and AIA materials.
  • Fractalish AI public overview.

Near-term priorities

  • Stronger replay and governed-execution visibility in BasinLab.
  • Cleaner public maps between Natural Math, CNTM, Fractalish, and Cognitive Basin.
  • More explicit mobile-native Fractalish AI architecture and traces.
  • Additional sensor and device experimentation on the Motorola path.
  • Better public contribution packaging for code, evidence, and serious critique.
  • A stable small-donation option.

Longer-term work

  • Dedicated public repos for additional source collections if and when publication posture supports them.
  • Stronger morphology-vs-generation benchmarks.
  • SymLan token criteria after descriptor stabilization.
  • InfinitySight perception work and richer APTD device embodiment.
  • Deeper native activation work beyond stock Android groundwork.
