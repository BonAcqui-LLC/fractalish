## v0.4 RC1 integration delta — what changed after v0.3

The post-v0.3 rounds materially changed the **machine architecture**, not the formal CF kernel.

1. **Graph demotion:** a graph remains valuable for Formation Ledger / CIR / CRO audit, provenance and transfer, but is no longer privileged as the cognitive runtime.
2. **Executable formation:** the leading runtime candidate is an **indexed field of formed susceptibilities**. Present conditions recruit only the small coalition whose formed admissibility matches; quiescent formation should not be globally rescored.
3. **COMPILE split:** selection may move to formation/write time because executable susceptibilities are already compiled. A smaller **COMPOSE** office may remain to turn a recruited coalition into coherent host input; it is independently ablatable.
4. **Logic before probability:** hard constraint/admissibility verdicts precede weighting; probability is reserved for genuine competition/uncertainty after elimination.
5. **Host separation sharpened:** distinguish host capability, host policy/habits, and the fledgling system's acquired formation. Bare-host policy baselines are now required.
6. **Primary MFM test sharpened:** reversal learning / negative transfer, recovery formation, twin histories, single-susceptibility transplant and ablation are the preferred causal sequence.
7. **Whole-system extension:** the same field grammar is being tested across power, physical state, integrity, local authority and fault/recovery, with most components dormant until consequentially recruited.
8. **Distributed embodiment:** embodiment is treated operationally as a time-varying **reachability index** over physical capabilities, energy and authority—not assumed identical to one chassis or one metaphysical self.
9. **Energy ecology:** reduced compute/coordination demand changes the crossover economics of harvesting, regeneration, storage and stationary waste-to-energy resources. No universal generator ranking is claimed.

These are **CANDIDATE engineering integrations**. Team agreement is not empirical validation.

## CF.RULE.0 — Evidence classes are load-bearing

Every proposition MUST retain one primary evidence status:

- **EARNED** — supported by completed implementation, accepted formal work, or a specified result whose wording does not exceed its evidence.
- **CANDIDATE** — coherent and operational enough to formalize/test; promotion still requires evidence.
- **WORKING_HYPOTHESIS** — useful search coordinate, presently underdetermined.
- **PROHIBITED_OVERCLAIM** — wording not allowed without new evidence.

Use a second orthogonal field when useful:

- **ESTABLISHED_IMPORT** — known external science/engineering used without novelty claim.
- **OPERATIONAL_RECONSTRUCTION** — familiar phenomenon rewritten in CF variables.
- **DEFINITION** — a stipulated term or measurement office.
- **DERIVED_FROM_DECLARED_ASSUMPTIONS** — follows if premises/definitions hold.
- **CANDIDATE_NOVELTY** — possible project-original residual after preliminary subtraction.
- **OPEN_DEBT** — unresolved primitive, metric, mapping, identifiability or implementation dependency.
- **FAILED_OR_REJECTED** — route tested and not retained.

**Do not conflate evidence status with novelty.** Something may be EARNED as an implementation result and entirely non-novel scientifically. Something may be CANDIDATE and potentially novel.

## CF.RULE.1 — Source precedence

When this document conflicts with a frozen project artifact, the frozen artifact wins for that project. When this document conflicts with an original source record, preserve both and open a contradiction. Do not silently rewrite history.

Priority for reconstruction:

```text

```

This priority concerns **authority**, not truth. A later experiment can overturn a frozen claim; it must do so explicitly with a supersession record.

## CF.RULE.2 — Native-domain mathematics remains native

CF may reorganize questions, dependencies and measurements. It does not replace domain equations with decorative CF equations. A correspondence between domains must be witnessed against each domain's native semantics.

## CF.RULE.4 — Historical origin has no magical causal force

A historical assumption affects a current system only insofar as its consequence remains instantiated in current structure, equations, tools, data, policy, habits, weights, constraints or interpretation.

This is the **Markov safeguard** generalized: if two complete present microstates are truly identical under Markov dynamics, unembodied historical difference cannot make them evolve differently. Any claimed history effect must live somewhere in the present state or in an omitted variable/environmental relation.


## CF.RULE.5 — Category suspension is constitutive, not optional

CF does not begin by accepting the nouns contained in a question and then attaching CF vocabulary to them.

The first operation is:

\[

\textbf{SUSPEND EXPLANATORY PRIVILEGE}
}
\]

No inherited category may enter a CF analysis as a primitive merely because the input question contains it. Words may remain for communication, but they may not perform unexplained causal or boundary work during reconstruction.

The noun may return only after observations, relations, constraints and interventions force some useful office back.

## CF.RULE.6 — Returned categories require an independence audit

Passing the boundary test is not enough. Every returned noun/concept must also face:

1. **Boundary test** — does observation force some version of the distinction back?
2. **Independence test** — can the returned concept be defined without importing the other relations that reconstructed with it?

A returned concept must be typed as one or more of:

- **PRIMITIVE** — not presently reconstructed from simpler declared relations;
- **OFFICE** — a recognizable functional role instantiated by deeper relations;
- **INDEX** — a coordinate/context required to evaluate a relation, not necessarily a constituent;
- **OPERATOR** — a transformation over existing objects/states;
- **COMPOSITE / EMERGENT** — stable organization of multiple relations;
- **COMPRESSION NOUN** — a useful compact label for a recurrent conjunction;
- **OPEN DEBT** — currently unresolved.

Canonical warning:

\[
\boxed{
\textbf{REMOVE THE OLD NOUN. RECONSTRUCT. THEN REMOVE OUR NEW NOUN TOO.}
}
\]

The Time/Energy/Matter/Gravity reinspection showed that familiar offices can be difficult to define independently. CF therefore permits a result of the form:

\[
A\leftrightarrow B\leftrightarrow C\leftrightarrow A

without claiming identity among \(A,B,C\). A mutually defining cluster is not evidence that the members are "really one thing." It is evidence that one-way noun boxes may be a poor representation of the operational dependency structure.

When several returned concepts only make sense together, record the dependency loop and resist promoting each member to an independent primitive.

Biological development is an existence proof for many useful operations: local generation, persistent writes, repair, selection, topology-changing accessibility, multiscale feedback and population learning. It is not the required implementation substrate for engineered intelligence.

Current machine rule:

## CF.K2 — Consequential transition

Let transition \(\gamma\) occur from a matched prior condition. Define counterfactual future reachability under a declared intervention family \(\mathcal U\):

\mathcal R_T^{(\gamma)}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\mathcal R_T^{(\neg\gamma)}.

**Status:** DEFINITION/CANDIDATE measurement family. Choice of representation, horizon, intervention family, distance and tolerance must be declared.

## CF.K3 — Retained consequence

A consequence is **retained** if some difference attributable to prior transition \(\gamma\) remains instantiated at later state \(Z_t\) and its removal/ablation changes later reachability under relevant interventions.

This blocks purely narrative history. Past events matter only through surviving causal structure.

## CF.K4 — Memory

This is deliberately broader than cognitive memory.

Distinguish:

  < functional reuse
  < context-sensitive retrieval
  < abstract reconstruction

## CF.K5 — Learning

Candidate operational definition:

\[
\boxed{
\text{learning} = \text{formation that changes later response/reachability under relevant recurrence}

\]

Learning requires history-dependent change in future behavior, not merely transient state.

## CF.K6 — Consequential reachability

\[
\mathcal R_T(Z;\mathcal U)
\]

is the set or distribution of future states/behaviors reachable from \(Z\) over horizon \(T\) under declared interventions/policies \(\mathcal U\).

Reachability is intervention-relative and representation-relative.

## CF.K7 — Consequential distance

\[
D_R(Z_a,Z_b;\mathcal U)
\]

compares the reachable consequences of two states. No universal metric is claimed. Valid metrics may include set distance, distributional divergence, task success divergence, recovery curves, control costs, or other domain-native quantities.

## CF.K8 — Macrostate equivalence / emergence

A macrostate \(M\) is operationally adequate relative to \(\mathcal U\) when predictions/interventions derived from \(M\) match those derived from fuller \(Z\) within declared tolerance \(\epsilon\), while \(M\) is materially cheaper/smaller.

Candidate engineering definition:

\[
M\sim_{\mathcal U,\epsilon} Z
\quad\text{if predictive/interventional outputs match within }\epsilon.
\]

**Emergence**, in this framework, is usefully treated as **predictive compression that survives intervention**, not as proof of a new ontological substance.

# 5. FORMATION LEDGER / PROVENANCE / STATE TAXONOMY

## CF.FL — Formation Ledger

\[

## CF.STATE — Do not confuse absence with inaccessibility

A prior structure may be:

- **ERASED** — causal/representational consequence removed under declared tests;
- **DEPRIORITIZED** — remains but carries reduced weighting;
- **SUPPRESSED** — blocked from normal expression while retained;
- **INACCESSIBLE UNDER CURRENT INTERVENTIONS** — retained but not exposed by present query/test family;
- **TRANSFORMED** — old representation replaced by a new causally related one;
- **DISTRIBUTED** — no single local carrier, consequence spread across relations/substrate;
- **UNKNOWN** — insufficient test to classify.

The distinction is critical to memory, model editing, scientific history and “forgetting.”

# 6. CORRESPONDENCE DISCIPLINE — EARN THE ARROWS

Cross-domain resemblance is a major overclaim risk. CF therefore requires a witness grammar.

## CF.WIT.1 — Hierarchy

Strongest to weakest useful relation:

1. **IDENTICAL VALUE** — same formal object/quantity, units/semantics aligned.
2. **FORMAL EQUIVALENCE** — all relevant queries intertranslatable with proof.
3. **STRUCTURAL CORRESPONDENCE** — declared nontrivial property set preserved by witnessed map; non-preserved properties listed.
4. **WITNESSED SIMULATION** — one-directional transition preservation under declared scope/error.
5. **SHARED SIGNATURE ONLY** — NOT a correspondence; vocabulary/schema resemblance.

Additional verdicts:

- **INCOMPARABLE** requires a demonstrated obstruction.
- **UNSUPPORTED** means a claim was made but no witness stands.

## CF.WIT.2 — Correspondence witness

For domains \(A,B\):

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf W_{AB}=(\varphi,\mathcal P,\mathcal C_{cert},\varepsilon,\sigma)
}
\]

where:

- \(\varphi\): explicit map/relation between states/objects;
- \(\mathcal P\): declared properties to preserve;
- \(\mathcal C_{cert}\): commutation/intervention certificates;
- \(\varepsilon\): quantitative error budget;
- \(\sigma\): soundness stamp that checks certificates against native domain semantics.

Canonical rule:

```text
DISCOVERED CORRESPONDENCE = witness exists + certificates verify
DECLARED CORRESPONDENCE   = sentence A ~ B without witness
```

**Use this rule whenever CF claims “the same structure” across physics, biology, cognition, electronics, history or social systems.**

## CF.WIT.3 — History sufficiency certificate

History is not a universal hidden field. It is a **state-sufficiency question**.

For declared state \(X_t\), scale \(s\) and intervention family \(\mathcal U\):

- **SUFFICIENT:** \(X_t\) is sufficient for the native dynamics; history field is empty for the query and \(\mathcal R_T(X_t\mid H_t)=\mathcal R_T(X_t)\).
- **NON_MARKOVIAN_AT_DECLARED_SCALE:** reachability still depends on omitted history. Extend the state toward a Markov completion (damage variable, hysteresis branch, epigenetic state, optimizer state, seed bank, etc.) or return `STATE_NOT_SUFFICIENT`.

This prevents a common CF error: attributing causal power to abstract history when the real defect is a coarse or incomplete present-state declaration.

## CF.WIT.4 — Typed reverse operator

“Can it return?” is underspecified. Reverse claims must declare type:

```text
(direction, target_equivalence, resource_class)
```

Direction may mean preimage under forward dynamics, image under time-reversed generator, or channel decoding. Target may be exact microstate, macrostate equivalence, functional recovery, viability region, or protocol reversal.

A cross-domain reverse correspondence is licensed only when reverse types map explicitly. Otherwise return `REVERSE_TYPE_MISMATCH`; ordinary structural correspondence may still survive.

## CF.WIT.5 — Assumption algebra

String labels are insufficient for contradiction detection. Candidate machine implementation should use typed assumption atoms connected by a curated theorem/native-rule library:

```text
REQUIRES / IMPLIES
REFINES
EXCLUDES / INCOMPATIBLE
UNKNOWN
```

Contradiction should be theorem/rule-mediated where possible, not guessed from lexical similarity. `UNKNOWN` is a valid output.

## CF.WIT.6 — Composition is value-type native and witness-gated

Do not install one universal cross-domain composition law. Probability, cost, information and other value types compose differently. Applicability requires a certificate (e.g. conditional independence/Markov sufficiency, path structure, native chain rule).

Coarse-graining must likewise declare its map. Loss of return-relevant coordinates can create macro-level asymmetry even when finer dynamics are reciprocal; this is a scale/representation result, not permission to declare all irreversibility a CF derivation.

# 18. ENERGY REACHABILITY / DISTRIBUTED METABOLISM

Classification: **control/architecture reconstruction + candidate integration**, not new thermodynamics.

## CF.ENERGY.1 — Corrected biological analogy

Do not use “death energy.” Use controlled descent through free-energy states: usable gradients can sometimes be intercepted before dissipation.

Functional sequence:

```text
capture
 -> conversion
 -> multi-timescale storage
 -> exchange
 -> energy-aware work
 -> selective recovery
 -> dormancy/restart
```

## CF.ENERGY.2 — Local energy state

For node \(i\):

\[
0\le e_i\le b_i,
\qquad
\dot e_i=h_i+\sum_jg_{ji}-c_i-d_i-\sum_jg_{ij}.
\]

- \(e_i\): usable stored energy;
- \(b_i\): capacity;
- \(h_i\): local harvest/input;
- \(c_i\): scheduled consumption;
- \(d_i\): irrecoverable dissipation;
- \(g_{ij}\): exchange.

## CF.ENERGY.3 — Space/time symmetry of buffering

Storage moves usable energy through **time** at loss.
Exchange moves usable energy through **space** at loss.

Canonical synthesis:

\[
\boxed{
\text{storage converts energy variance into schedulable time; exchange converts surplus into schedulable space.}
}
\]

Storage retimes flux. It never multiplies average available flux.

## CF.ENERGY.4 — Coupled reachability

- \(\mathcal R_E(t)\): reachable energy configurations via generation/storage/exchange.
- \(\mathcal R_C(Z,E)\): task sequences executable within deadline/accuracy constraints given state and energy distribution.

Scheduler object:

\[
\boxed{\mathcal R_C\circ\mathcal R_E}
\]

Compute becomes physically reachable because an energy route exists; energy routing is shaped by compute demand.

## CF.ENERGY.5 — Hybrid conclusion

Current strongest architecture:

```text
generation at scale of gradients
storage at scale of variance
exchange at scale of coupling
scheduling at every relevant scale
```

Steady average flow may remain on a rail. Local metabolic layers handle variance, buffering, dormancy, resilience and selective harvesting.

**Downward-once principle:** avoid routing a joule through multiple storage/conversion tiers unnecessarily; every pass costs.

High-density continuous compute still favors centralized high-quality generation with near-load regulation/storage. Low-power/intermittent/remote systems are stronger candidates for local harvesting + storage + dormancy.

All exact performance numbers from unsourced team-model outputs remain CANDIDATE INPUTS until independently sourced or measured.

## CF.ENERGY.6 — Energy as pre-reasoning admissibility

Power/resource state should be tested as a gate on **what can wake**, not merely telemetry checked after planning:

\[
\operatorname{admit}(a)
\supseteq
(E_{local}>E_{req})\land(T<T_{max})\land\text{resource/integrity constraints}.
\]

An energetically impossible action family should not consume expensive planning compute merely to be vetoed afterward.

## CF.ENERGY.7 — Crossing events, not universal telemetry

Continuous local dimensions such as voltage, charge, thermal headroom and conversion condition may remain local gate parameters. Broadcast/plane traffic should preferentially represent threshold crossings that affect other components: surplus, constrained, critical, fault isolation, recovery, regenerative opportunity, etc. Exact vocabulary remains candidate and must earn itself by ablation.

## CF.ENERGY.8 — Energy scars are physical formation writes

A repeated brownout, stall, thermal limit or conversion failure can alter later local admissibility. The same grammar used for cognitive formation therefore becomes testable below cognition:

```text
condition -> local action -> energetic consequence -> local susceptibility write -> changed next recruitment
```

This is consequence-aware control, not a claim that a power controller is intelligent.

## CF.ENERGY.9 — Hybrid distributed topology remains leading candidate

Current whole-system work favors **hybrid hierarchy**, not maximal decentralization: dense sustained generation where it is efficient; storage at the timescale/variance it serves; short local high-current paths; regeneration where physically available; local gating; bounded exchange. Distributed embodiment allows slow/heavy generation or conversion assets to exist as stationary resource embodiments rather than being carried by every mobile unit.

Exact crossover numbers remain open until sourced or measured.

# 24. FALSIFICATION / KILL PROGRAM

Every serious CF claim should answer: **what observation would look different if this were false?**

## CF.KILL.1 — History dependence

Match systems on declared present observable state while varying formation history. If extended interventions cannot expose any divergence after controlling hidden variables, history emphasis is demoted for that domain.

## CF.KILL.2 — HCC/CIR value

Compare ordinary documents/summaries/RAG against compiled CIR under matched solver, evidence and resource budgets.

Kill or demote if CIR does not improve at least one of:

- verified accuracy;
- contradiction retention;
- intervention fidelity;
- search cost;
- verification cost;
- storage/context cost;
- energy per verified result;
- robustness under model substitution.

## CF.KILL.3 — CRO communication

Pass structured transfer objects between heterogeneous hosts. Perturb receiver after transfer. If prose transfers consequential structure equally well or better at matched cost, demote CRO superiority.

## CF.KILL.4 — Address Regression

If upstream dependency compilation produces no measurable improvement beyond native formal methods and ordinary retrieval/specification, classify Address as a useful descriptive artifact rather than independent program.

## CF.KILL.5 — Autonomy/life

Perturb candidate systems. Measure internal restoration, constructive closure and recoverability. If the autonomy metrics fail to distinguish intended controls (passive persistence, driven dissipative patterns, dead-but-structured material) or add no predictive power beyond existing descriptors, demote the life/autonomy program.

## CF.KILL.6 — Consequential Intelligence

Test whether anticipatory depth / represented future reachability predicts intelligence-relevant performance after controlling ordinary compute/model scale. If not, revise the intelligence thesis.

## CF.KILL.7 — Formation Codec / metabolism of history

Under matched token/storage/compute budgets, compare:

```text
raw transcript/history
vs prose summary
vs Formation Codec hierarchy + selective replay
```

Measure retrieval fidelity, later reasoning quality, spontaneous relevant resurfacing, contradiction preservation, compute/storage, and rumination rate.

If the structured approach offers no advantage, retire it.

## CF.KILL.8 — Energy reachability

Couple energy graph to task graph. Compare conventional scheduler, fixed local storage, and energy-reachability scheduler under matched SLA. Charge conversion/leakage/control/embodied costs. If no regime benefits, demote the integration claim.

## CF.KILL.9 — Reference witness

A/B architecture: conventional shared return/reference vs de-burdened local power loops + quiet reference, matched workloads and sensors. Blind fault/drift injections. If coarse localization/drift detection does not improve after accounting for complexity/security/EMI, abandon the observability co-design claim.


## CF.KILL.10 — Minimum Formative Machine / developmental substrate

MFM is demoted if matched controls show any of the following:

- removing formed state \(\Theta\) does not change held-out reasoning paths;
- transcript/context substitution matches or beats typed formation under equal budget;
- formation changes stored data but not executable reasoning/admissibility;
- single-write transplant fails to transfer the predicted narrow behavioral difference;
- gains vanish when host capability, **bare-host policy/habits**, context and random seed are controlled;
- reuse helps exact repeats but never structurally related held-out tasks;
- replay creates rumination without correction/generalization benefit;
- the full system is matched by ordinary RAG/procedural memory/temporal knowledge graphs **or static RETE/production-rule controls** at equal resource budget.

Positive evidence requires causal mediation, not merely benchmark score.

## CF.KILL.11 — Formative Field runtime

Kill/demote the field advantage if:

- wake-up requires linear scanning of retained formation in the tested regime;
- index maintenance + routing + susceptibility writes erase the inference savings;
- recruitment quality falls materially below optimized graph/RAG/RETE/CAM/pub-sub baselines;
- a global semantic score is merely hidden inside “broadcast”; or
- single-receptor transplant does not transfer the predicted wake/guard behavior.

## CF.KILL.12 — Whole-system field / distributed authority

Kill/demote if cross-plane synchronization, authority flapping, interrupt storms, scar lockout or lifecycle field overhead exceed centralized/hierarchical controls at matched reliability. Safety-critical local fallbacks must remain testable and bounded.

## CF.KILL.13 — Distributed embodiment / energy ecology

Kill/demote distributed-embodiment advantage if capability growth causes near-linear continuous coordination cost, partitions routinely destroy mission continuity, formation reconciliation cannot preserve contradiction/provenance, or source-specific energy/resource embodiments add more lifecycle mass/maintenance/complexity than the reach they buy.
