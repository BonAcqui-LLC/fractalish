It did not convert all energy, storage, or robotics work into AI. Flyshaft KESS remains adjacent only; Q* remains uncertain; the power assay remains data evidence without an AI conclusion.

A bridge is not a dependency. Energy may constrain reachable computation without being intelligence. A sensor fabric may support perception without authorizing action. A material may retain history without becoming a mind.
