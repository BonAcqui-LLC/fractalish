1. Bare-host profile

Measure capability and host policy before formation. If the host already supplies the effect, acquired formation is not identified.

2. Reversal / negative transfer

Train one history, reverse the contingency, and predict where prior formation should initially hurt.

3. Recovery formation

Test whether subsequent formation produces faster or structurally different recovery.

4. Narrow transplant

Move one validated susceptibility or bounded formed object and predict the transferred difference.

5. Ablation

Remove the same object and require the predicted effect to disappear.

6. Matched baselines

Compare quality, latency, compute, storage, update, correction, and audit costs across native alternatives.
