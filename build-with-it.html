Can experience become executable formation?

Minimum Formative Machine v0.1

A typed formed state is compiled through independently ablatable BRIEF, GUARDS, and COSTS channels; typed witnessed consequence governs later formation updates. The test is whether changing formation measurably changes later reasoning or admissibility under matched controls.

Can knowledge move without flattening into prose?

Structured consequential transfer

Formation Ledger, historical compilation, intermediate representation, reachability objects, and Consequential Fidelity investigate whether formed state can transfer with provenance, contradiction, uncertainty, and testable future consequences intact.

Can transient cognition become compact, reconstructable history?

Formation Codec

Periodically encode active concepts, relations, salience, uncertainty, contradictions, open questions, provenance, time, and trajectory. Reconstruction must never fabricate detail.

CANDIDATE ARCHITECTURE

Can a machine witness history written into its substrate?

Physical self-witness

Thermal history, impedance drift, wear, component aging, energy state, and fault propagation become machine-usable distinctions for a proposed perception-and-evidence intake layer.

OPTIONAL ENGINEERING BRANCH

Can morphology retain useful consequential history?

Carbon nanotube morphology memory

CNTM asks whether growth history can become durable morphology and conductance, and eventually whether retained physical geometry can alter later computation. It is not required for MFM.

Can a quieter reference become an observability surface?

Reference fabric

Separate energy, information, and reference roles. Explore coarse localization, degradation detection, transfer-function drift, passive sensing, and bounded probing without claiming fine tomography.

CANDIDATE ENGINEERING DIRECTION

How does energy alter reachable computation?

Energy reachability

Storage converts variance into schedulable time; exchange converts surplus into schedulable space; the rail remains the average-flow channel. Centralized power was not disproved.

BOUNDED SYNTHESIS

Where does a machine's body end?

Body and scale

A candidate body boundary follows physical resources incorporated into actionable closure: sensors, tools, vehicles, fabrication, remote compute, and infrastructure.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Can new formation expand material reachability?

Recursive disassembly

Waste is treated as currently unreachable material. Better tools and knowledge may reduce unrecoverable residual; they do not repeal entropy or promise literal zero waste.
