Can a quieter reference become an observability surface?

Reference fabric

Separate energy, information, and reference roles. Explore coarse localization, degradation detection, transfer-function drift, passive sensing, and bounded probing without claiming fine tomography.

CANDIDATE ENGINEERING DIRECTION

How does energy alter reachable computation?

Energy ecology

Generation, storage, conversion, regeneration, and exchange are compared against the machine's actual load profile. No source is privileged, and no universal generator ranking is claimed.

ACTIVE CANDIDATE BRANCH

Which formed capabilities are physically reachable now?

Distributed embodiment

The candidate embodiment index tracks reachable capability, energy, bounded authority, local formation, location, and connectivity. A body is not assumed to equal one chassis or one metaphysical self.

What happens when embodiments partition and rejoin?

Local formation and reconciliation

Inaccessible is not erased. Local formation may diverge during a partition; rejoining requires provenance-aware validation, contradiction, supersession, forking, or quarantine.
