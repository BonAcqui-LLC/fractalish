CF's first operation

Suspend the inherited category. Keep the evidence.

Category suspension is constitutive of Consequential Formation, not an optional lens beside it.

Human knowledge is a map drawn on the cave wall.

Some lines mark real cliffs. Some mark useful trails. Some carry historical assumptions or compress several phenomena into one label. CF temporarily withholds a line's explanatory privilege and looks at the territory again.

A noun that was never removed cannot be said to have survived the CF filter. Removing a word does not remove observations, ban ordinary language, or prove the category false.

The category must earn its way back twice

  1. Boundary test

    Does observation or intervention force some version of the distinction back?

  2. Independence test

    Can the returned concept be defined without importing the other relations reconstructed with it?

  3. Type the return

    Classify it as PRIMITIVE, OFFICE, INDEX, OPERATOR, COMPOSITE / EMERGENT, COMPRESSION NOUN, or OPEN DEBT.

  4. Test the consequence

    State a prediction, intervention, native comparison, or kill condition.

Remove the old noun. Reconstruct. Then remove our new noun too.

Rules that keep the method honest

Keep native mathematics

Removing a noun is not permission to flatten domain-specific equations or discard established controls.

Permit incomparability and no change

Two domains may share a signature while lacking a valid mapping. INCOMPARABLE, UNSUPPORTED, METHOD FAILED, and NO CHANGE are legitimate results.

Audit our vocabulary too

Reachability, closure, access, recoverability, formation, constraint, and distinction are not immune from the same test.
