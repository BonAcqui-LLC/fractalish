# RFC-0001: Constitutional Foundation

Status: CANONICAL-CANDIDATE FOUNDATION
Date: 2026-08-09

## Summary

This RFC records the August 2026 Fractalish reframing:

```text
geometry
  -> geometry as receipt
  -> accumulated form
  -> generative grammar
  -> first verb
  -> future reachability
  -> maintaining relation
  -> intervention
  -> maintainer recursion
```

The constitutional center is:

> Form is accumulated consequence.

> Geometry is one way of measuring the receipt.

> Fractalish seeks the generative grammar upstream of accumulated form.

## Lineage

Earlier Fractalish public language emphasized exploration leaving form behind. That remains valuable for bounded path-dependent systems. This RFC generalizes the center: exploration is one possible mechanism of accumulation, not the universal definition.

The new framing preserves process forensics as a method. It no longer treats process forensics as the whole identity of the program.

## Key Commitments

- Representation plurality: geometry, morphology, topology, spectra, graphs, manifolds, state spaces, phase spaces, dynamics, and traces are instruments.
- Residue accounting: every representation preserves some relations and loses others.
- First-verb restraint: GO / STOP / HOLD and PERMIT / INHIBIT / PRESERVE remain hypotheses, not established universal primitives.
- Intervention standard: description reads the receipt; intervention tests the grammar.
- Desiloization: decompartmentalization without decontextualization.
- Epistemic restraint: never pretend to know the unknowable; never call the unresolved resolved.
- Observation boundary: unobserved does not mean zero.

## Claim Boundary

This RFC does not claim a universal grammar of nature, a universal geometry, a proof that everything is fractal, a machine-consciousness result, a universal decoder, or causal inference from morphology alone.
