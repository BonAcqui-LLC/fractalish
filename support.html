Support priorities

Engineering time

Cognitive Basin, replay, governed execution, device work, and mobile-native implementation all benefit from sustained technical focus.

Source curation and public maps

Natural Math, CNTM, and related Fractalish material span many artifacts. Curating, reconciling, and explaining them takes real time.

Device experimentation

Phone activation, sensor work, safe hardware testing, and native bring-up require careful, iterative effort.

Public documentation

Outside developers can only help if the work is documented clearly enough to enter without guesswork.

Immediate forms of support

  • Contribute through the public repos.
  • Send serious technical critique or architecture feedback.
  • Offer Android, sensor, kernel, or deterministic-systems expertise.
  • Reach out about sponsorship, research support, or collaboration.
