  • Stock Android app builds and runs as llc.bonacqui.aia version 0.1.1-stockos.

What the read-only topology work found

The preserved Motorola activation evidence includes stock closure work, offline topology derivation, and a live read-only topology attempt. The read-only work remained disciplined: when the required device-presence and authorization gates did not pass, it failed safe rather than guessing or forcing transitions.

The preserved A02B summary is explicitly FAILED_SAFE: no live device was authorized over ADB, no fastboot topology was captured, no fastbootd attempt was made, and no reboot transition was performed.

Why this matters

The phone work is the bridge between Fractalish as a framework and Fractalish AI as a real embodied activation path. It is where sensor access, local persistence, bounded review, and hardware constraints stop being abstract architecture notes and become engineering reality.
