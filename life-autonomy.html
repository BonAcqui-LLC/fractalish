Life without starting from "life"

When does an arrangement begin maintaining the conditions for its own continuation?

That question turns a familiar noun into an operational research program.

Why we asked

The label arrives before the mechanism.

Calling something living identifies a useful class, but does not by itself specify which organizational operations produce the transition.

What survived

Self-maintenance, restoration, closure, and viable routes.

A living organization may be understood as one whose ongoing processes help maintain or reconstruct the conditions required for those processes to continue.

Candidate reconstruction

Life ≈ self-maintaining constraint closure

This is a candidate operational framework, not settled biology. It must be connected to independently measurable variables and compared against existing theories of living organization.

The organism boundary

The organism may be the closure, not the components.

Atoms are continually replaced. What persists is an organization of mutually maintaining processes and constraints. The open question is whether an organism's boundary should follow its skin or the closure of dependencies required to maintain it.

An autonomy handoff occurs when responsibility for maintaining a required constraint shifts from an external source toward the organization itself. It can be partial and multidimensional.

Origin and death on one map

Origin

Internally maintained viable reachability appears or grows.

Life

The organization reconstructs conditions that keep viable routes open.

Damage

Reachability narrows, but recovery routes remain.

Dormancy

Activity approaches zero while recoverable organization remains.

Death

No declared route returns the organization to viable self-maintenance.

"No route" is relative to stated resources, interventions, horizon, and model. This is an operational hypothesis, not a universal mathematical solution to death.

What would kill it

The closure must add discriminating power.

The proposal weakens if closure variables cannot be measured, fail to distinguish matched living and nonliving systems, or add no prediction or intervention advantage beyond existing biological accounts.
