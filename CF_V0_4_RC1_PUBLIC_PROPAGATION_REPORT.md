## What Was Propagated

- The homepage now identifies CF v0.4 RC1 as the current canonical candidate and gives its architectural correction first-page visibility.
- The Formation Ledger is presented as the candidate audit, provenance, transfer, debugging, and causal-credit representation, not a presumed runtime.
- The Formative Field is presented as a build-test target: indexed formed susceptibilities, hard admissibility before probability, sparse recruitment, and optional independently ablatable `COMPOSE?`.
- Host capability, host policy or habits, and acquired formation are separated; bare-host profiling is required.
- The primary MFM test sequence is reversal and negative transfer, recovery, narrow transplant, ablation, and matched native baselines.
- Distributed embodiment is presented as changing physical reachability across capability, energy, authority, local formation, location, and connectivity, not identity across bodies.
- Energy ecology is presented without a universal energy-source ranking.
- Machine-speed developmental search remains a legitimate engineering argument, while fixed evolutionary-time claims remain prohibited.

These changes were propagated through the homepage, Framework, Start Here, What Emerged, Memory and Intelligence, Cognitive Basin, Build With It, Project Map, Documents, Evidence, Status, For Reviewers, Scientific Neighbors, What CF Does Not Claim, Try to Kill It, shared context/footer generation, repository orientation, and search.

## Claims Not Promoted

- RC1 is not frozen, universally validated, or a declaration of discovery.
- MFM v0.1 and the Formative Field are specifications and build-test targets, not achieved intelligence.
- A graph or ledger is not called thought merely because it records formation.
- Distributed capability does not establish one identity across multiple bodies.
- No universal hierarchy of energy sources is claimed.
- Biology, cells, molecular chemistry, metabolism, biological development, and CNTM are not machine-architecture requirements.
- Mutual constitution is not identity, and `time = energy = matter = gravity` is not published.
- Native baselines and native-domain authority retain priority.
