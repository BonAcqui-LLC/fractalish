What Consequential Formation does not claim

Removing a category for inspection is not the same as disproving it.

Time is unreal.

Energy is unreal.

Matter is unreal.

CF is new physics.

Life has been formally redefined.

Death is mathematically solved.

Consciousness has been solved.

All matter is intelligent.

Current artificial intelligence is alive.

Biology and machinery are identical.

History acts without a present carrier.

Analogy establishes equivalence.

Centralized power is obsolete.

Electrical ground is literally unnecessary.

Identity survives substrate transfer automatically.

Every inherited noun is wrong.

The positive rule: keep the observations, keep native mathematics, identify present carriers, declare interventions and resources, preserve incomparability, and redraw every boundary the evidence requires.

Why limits increase the value of the work

A framework that cannot report a negative result is not conducting a test. CF becomes useful only where it exposes measurable variables, produces discriminating interventions, improves engineering, or cleanly returns an inherited category to its prior office.
