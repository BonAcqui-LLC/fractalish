## Result

Fractalish now has a first-class AI public laboratory rooted at `/ai`. It presents the machine-intelligence program as mixed-maturity research: frozen narrow implementations, tested infrastructure, active experiments, specifications, historical lineage, negative results, publication holds, and unresolved bridges. It does not present MFM, Formative Field, distributed embodiment, physical substrate work, or heterogeneous AI collaboration as achieved intelligence or validation of Consequential Formation.

The public center of gravity is the distinction `CAPABILITY != HOST POLICY != ACQUIRED FORMATION`. The new section asks whether experience can leave executable consequence that causally changes what the same profiled host later notices, rejects, trusts, retrieves, tests, recovers, or reaches.

## Access Limits

This was a broad functional audit, not a forensic image of every byte on every volume. Encrypted, cloud-only, disconnected, permission-denied, deleted, unmounted, and third-party account content was not available. The designated R2R verbicentric source root was empty. Duplicate backup/worktree trees were sampled for lineage rather than exhaustively reread. Model caches, application folders, and unrelated private domains were excluded by design.
