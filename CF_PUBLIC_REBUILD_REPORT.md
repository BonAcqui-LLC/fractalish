# CF Public Rebuild Report

Date: 2026-09-12

## Outcome

The newcomer narrative was rebuilt as a causal journey:

`inherited category -> temporary removal -> preserved evidence -> reconstructed relations -> tested boundary -> Consequential Formation -> implications -> engineering -> falsification`

Consequential Formation no longer appears as unexplained front-door terminology. Project names and acronyms appear only after their motivating questions.

## Routes Replaced

- `/` - now begins with the category-removal experiment and cave-wall safeguard.
- `/start-here` - now tells the story of the documented rounds and their bounded outcomes.
- `/consequential-formation` - now follows the rounds and provides plain language, technical objects, status, and kill conditions.
- `/project-map` - now groups projects by research question and independent evidence state.

## New Routes

- `/erase-the-nouns` - method and interactive entry point.
- `/rounds` - major boundary rounds using removed/survived/changed/status structure.
- `/what-emerged` - implication gateway.
- `/life-autonomy` - closure, organism boundary, autonomy handoff, viable reachability, dormancy, and death.
- `/memory-intelligence` - retained consequence, learning, intelligence ladder, consciousness boundary, and identity boundary.
- `/cognition` - newcomer explanation of Cognitive Basin, uncertainty, memory metabolism, inhibition, and bounded maintenance.
- `/reinspect-knowledge` - language, history, structured transfer, Address Regression, and representation debt.
- `/build-with-it` - engineering branches introduced by question before project name.
- `/what-cf-does-not-claim` - explicit prohibited overclaims.
- `/try-to-kill-it` - evidence classes, correspondence discipline, and framework-level kill conditions.
