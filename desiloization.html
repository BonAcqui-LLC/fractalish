Public vocabulary note · June 2026

Desiloizing Geometry

The geometry was in front of us. The silos made it invisible.

Fractalish is not claiming all fractals are identical. It is building a shared comparison language for recurring geometric process signals across domains.

Desiloization is the act of reconnecting knowledge that institutions separated for convenience, funding, credentialing, or historical accident.

Fractalish desiloizes geometry by giving roads, rivers, retinas, roots, cracks, dendrites, lightning, tumors, chips, signals, and soils a shared comparison language.

The math was not missing. The bridge was missing.

Guardrails

  • Similarity is not identity.
  • Fractalish does not force fractal readings.
  • AMCVA marks absence, obscuration, erasure, non-diagnostic cases, competing geometry, complementary geometry, and capture artifacts.
  • Cross-domain resemblance is a hypothesis generator until validated by process metadata, temporal change, or outcome linkage.

Invitation

If your field has already named one of these geometries, bring the name. If your field has data, bring the data. If our interpretation fails, submit the failure. Proof-negative cases are as valuable as proof-positive cases because AMCVA is how the system refuses false geometry.

Follow @fractalish_com for dataset calls, morphology challenges, tool releases, and public cross-domain comparison tests.

Canonical fractals become the alphabet. Physical morphology becomes the language.
