Try the Lens

Use CF before learning the machinery.

Pick an object, system, problem, theory, organism, machine, institution, or question. Then walk through what changed and what that change made reachable.

Choose a system and answer the prompts below. The lens is a worksheet, not a verdict.

Rule of use

Do not begin by naming the thing as life, intelligence, memory, waste, body, or machine. Begin with observable change. Add names only after the relations earn them.

Fast examples

Path across grass

Change: footsteps. Remainder: compressed ground. Constraint: easier walking along the same route. Falsifier: later walkers show no measurable route bias under matched conditions.

Scientific concept

Change: an assumption enters a field's notation. Remainder: methods and problem statements inherit it. Constraint: some questions become easier to ask than others.

Machine service

Change: failures and repairs accumulate. Remainder: logs, patches, dependencies, and operator habits. Constraint: future behavior depends on that retained history.

Good output

A good CF reading produces a testable statement, not a poetic label. It should say what remained, what changed about reachability, and what evidence would force revision.
