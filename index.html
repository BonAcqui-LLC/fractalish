Fractalish is a text-first, trace-first, and video-supported public research portal for studying whether observable form can preserve partial evidence about the processes that produced it.

A video may introduce the idea. The text must carry the idea. The trace must test the idea. No knowledge should be trapped inside linear video.

The work connects Fractalish as process forensics, Natural Math as a bounded local transition grammar, MCVA / AMCVA / HOLD as a disciplined vocabulary, and Fractalish Commons as the open testing surface for cross-domain morphology comparison.

What this is

A public research program and working comparison language for process from form, morphology as memory, decision points as data points, and cross-domain geometry study.

The world may already have recorded the evidence. It simply did not know what it was looking at.

What this is not

It is not a claim that all morphology is readable, that all patterns are fractal, that Natural Math models every domain, or that one metric can diagnose a process.

A fractal reading is not forced. It must be earned. Similarity is not identity. HOLD exists to prevent false closure.

Current framework lanes

Fractalish

Fractalish is process forensics: the disciplined attempt to read preserved process from geometry, surface change, branching, scarring, wake structure, and recovery marks.

Natural Math

Natural Math is the forward or generative layer. It asks what forms finite, local, energy-bounded systems tend to make under constraint, memory, and recovery.

Field guide

MCVA / AMCVA / HOLD

MCVA reads where morphology preserves process. AMCVA records where it does not safely speak. HOLD preserves uncertainty when the evidence is not enough yet.
