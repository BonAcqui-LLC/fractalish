Documents are the primary product.

Fractalish is a public research program studying whether observable form can preserve partial evidence about the processes that produced it.

The current work connects process forensics in external morphology, Natural Math as a bounded local transition grammar, Cognitive Basin as an instrumented process field, and MCVA / AMCVA / HOLD as a disciplined vocabulary for signal, non-signal, and justified uncertainty.

The public posture is intentionally narrow: open to review, critique, and replication; careful about claim boundaries; and explicit that unresolved cases should remain unresolved.

Read the current documents View the framework Review guide

What this is

A document-first research home for process from form, morphology as memory, decision points as data points, and claim-disciplined cross-domain comparison.

Fractalish is process forensics. Cognitive Basin is the instrumented process field. Natural Math is the local transition grammar. MCVA is the vocabulary. HOLD is the restraint against false closure.

What this is not

It is not a consciousness claim, a medical diagnostic product, a regulatory standard, a universal law, or a claim that all morphology is readable.

AMCVA records when morphology does not safely speak. Similarity is not identity. Fractal dimension is one descriptor, not a diagnosis.
