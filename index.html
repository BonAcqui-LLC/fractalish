Why outside help matters now

Implementation help

We need collaborators who care about deterministic execution, replayable state, small-run acceptance, Android-native implementation, local-first storage, schema discipline, and claim boundaries.

Scientific and mathematical critique

Natural Math, CNTM, Fractalish, and Cognitive Basin all benefit from hard criticism. Proof-negative cases, broken assumptions, better formalisms, and sharper boundary conditions are useful contributions.

Device and native systems work

The Motorola lane is a real bridge between theory and embodiment. Kernel, sensor, Android internals, and boot-chain literacy are especially valuable now.

Documentation and curation

The stack is large. People who can help improve maps, explain components, reconcile variants, and keep provenance honest make the implementation easier for everyone else to enter.

Public claim boundary

Fractalish now names Fractalish AI and BasinLab publicly because the build effort is real and active. That does not mean the site is claiming proven sentience, artificial personhood, medical authority, or a universal decoder. The public standard remains the same: explicit evidence states, explicit uncertainty, and no flattening of unresolved work into slogans.

Support the work

If you want to help directly, the fastest lanes today are technical collaboration, hostile review, source curation, and sponsorship interest. A small-donation support rail is now being added to the site so the work can ask for practical help as openly as it asks for intellectual help.
