Privacy and attribution

Default public attribution is entity-level.

Fractalish uses organization-level public attribution by default. Exact personal and AI provenance remains preserved privately unless a release explicitly opts in to individual credit.

Default wording

Fractalish Research Program, stewarded by BonAcqui LLC.

Not public by default

No personal names, aliases, biographies, family relationships, locations, personal contact details, or possessive author branding should appear in the current public tree unless explicitly approved for that exact release.

Private provenance

Private project records may retain exact human and AI provenance, contribution history, and pre-release attribution notes.

Approval boundary

Prior release approval does not grant blanket future consent for personal attribution in later releases.

Entity-level attribution in public. Exact provenance in private records.
