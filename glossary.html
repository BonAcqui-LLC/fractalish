Glossary

Canonical public definitions.

These are the shortest usable definitions for public review. For fuller placement inside the stack, continue to the project map and research package.

Ageometrics

The proposed quantitative study of what a specified geometric representation fails to preserve relative to the fuller available record.

Geometric Sufficiency Ratio

The proposed task-relative ratio measuring the fraction of recoverable target-relevant improvement captured by a declared geometry.

Non-Geometric Residue

The normalized performance gap left by the declared geometry relative to the fuller available record.

Interventional GSR

The GSR variant measured against intervention-response targets rather than passive observation alone.

Temporal GSR

The GSR variant for targets that depend on developmental or event history after history is compressed into form.

Representation-Stability Envelope

The spread of GSR values across a declared family of admissible geometric representations.

Geometric Encoding Cost

The declared cost of adding dimensions, labels, bits, or auxiliary fields to improve geometric sufficiency.

Minimal Residue-Restoring Channel

The smallest auxiliary channel that restores performance above a declared sufficiency threshold.

Blind Growth Memory Hypothesis

The proposal that locally informed, path-dependent development can embody part of encounter history in final structure.

Perceptual Horizon

The extent of information available to an updating process beyond its current local state.

Geometry-History Tradeoff

The proposed frontier between broader foresight and recoverable developmental history preserved in form.

MCVA / AMCVA / HOLD

The readout vocabulary, guardrail atlas, and unresolved evidentiary state used across the broader Fractalish stack.
