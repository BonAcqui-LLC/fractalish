What emerged

The old nouns became new questions, not new unquestioned primitives.

The rounds did not erase life, memory, or intelligence. They exposed operations those words may compress, then required every returned concept to face an independence audit.

01

Life and autonomy

Closure is currently a composite; recoverability is an intervention-indexed operator, not a solved universal boundary.

02

Memory and intelligence

Retained consequence, learning, anticipation, and the distinction between host capability and acquired formation.

03

Cognition and MFM

Can typed formed history compile into constraints that causally change what the same capable host reasons next?

These are operational reconstructions and research hypotheses. They do not announce settled replacements for biology, neuroscience, cognitive science, physics, or philosophy.
