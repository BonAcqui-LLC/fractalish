What emerged

The old nouns became new questions.

The rounds did not erase life, memory, or intelligence. They exposed the operations those words may be compressing.

01

Life and autonomy

Self-maintaining closure, autonomy handoff, viable reachability, dormancy, and death.

02

Memory and intelligence

Retained consequence, learning, adaptation, anticipation, and identity.

03

Cognition and the Basin

Uncertainty, persistent formation, memory metabolism, inhibition, and bounded maintenance.

These are operational reconstructions and research hypotheses. They do not announce settled replacements for biology, neuroscience, cognitive science, or philosophy.
