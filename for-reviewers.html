A short path through the claims and record.

Fractalish studies how past change becomes present structure and changes what can happen next. CF begins by suspending inherited categories, reconstructing consequential structure, auditing whether returned concepts can stand independently, and typing what returns.

What is currently strongest?

  • The v0.3 method explicitly suspends inherited categories, preserves native evidence, audits independence, and types returned concepts.
  • The cross-domain relation remains a candidate framework, not a universal law.
  • Several separate software artifacts have frozen or locally validated evidence under named protocols.

Full v0.3 candidate | Compact packet

What is frozen or implemented?

  • Natural Math v5: frozen integer baseline under its declared fixtures.
  • Authority Compiler: verified finite reference fragment.
  • Bolt-On: frozen v0.3 evidence and locally validated v0.4 Stage 1 host contract, not production integration.

What remains candidate?

  • CF v0.3 as a methodological, representational, and engineering unification framework.
  • Minimum Formative Machine v0.1 as a specification and immediate build-test target, not an achieved intelligence.
  • Address Regression and UFWK beyond their named evidence.
  • The complete Natural Math, Cognitive Basin, FormationCore, Persistent Observer, and host-integration sequence.
  • CNTM and physical morphology memory as optional substrate research, not an MFM prerequisite.

What failed or was demoted?

  • Resonant Morphology M1 is retained as a historical development artifact, not flagship proof.
  • Construction A+ preserves a descriptor collision rather than claiming unique reconstruction.
  • Unresolved cases remain HOLD; resemblance alone is not discovery.

What fields already explain parts?

Start with Scientific Neighbors, then inspect what CF does not claim and the falsification conditions.

What should a skeptical scientist inspect?

  • Native-field preservation and correspondence witnesses.
  • The four-noun calibration and unresolved physics debts.
  • Negative/null controls, evidence status, and MFM kill conditions.
  • The prohibition on consciousness, replacement-physics, identity, and fixed-timeline claims.
