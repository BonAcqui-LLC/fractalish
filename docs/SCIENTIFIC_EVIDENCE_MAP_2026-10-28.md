## Evidence-class firewall

- **Direct experimental evidence** supports only the measured system, intervention, resolution, and reported uncertainty.
- **Computational modeling** can expose consequences of assumptions and generate predictions; it does not substitute for the measurements it models.
- **Mathematical derivation** establishes results inside declared axioms/conditions; physical correspondence must be separately tested.
- **Perspective and interpretation** can organize research questions; persuasive framing is not an experimental result.
- **Team research synthesis** generates candidates and criticism; model agreement is not independent empirical evidence.
