## Claim vocabulary

- **Natural Math process operators:** EXTEND / SENSE / RESTRICT.
- **Cognitive Basin action states:** EXTEND / HOLD / RETRACT.
- **Cognitive Basin evidence postures:** SUPPORTED / UNRESOLVED / CONTRADICTED.
- **MCVA boundary:** a target-relative sufficiency test, not proof of identity or unique history.
- **AMCVA role:** record what is absent, erased, unrecoverable, or still outside the declared representation.
- **Specificity receipt:** target-contract compliance, not absolute truth.
- **Ageometrics frame:** observer, protocol, representation, and target must be declared.
- **Cognitive Basin boundary:** inspectable persistent state, not a consciousness claim.
- **Generator boundary:** leaf-like output is one controlled fixture; it does not define Fractalish or serve as the public thesis.
