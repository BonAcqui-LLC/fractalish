# Public Attribution Policy

Default public attribution:

`Fractalish Research Program, stewarded by BonAcqui LLC.`

## Public defaults

- No personal names, aliases, biographies, family relationships, locations, or personal contact details by default.
- No person-specific possessive phrasing in public release copy by default.
- No public team profile without explicit approval for that exact release.

## Private provenance

- Exact human and AI provenance may remain preserved in private project records.
- Earlier approval for one release does not grant blanket approval for later releases.
