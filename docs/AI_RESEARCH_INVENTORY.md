# Fractalish AI Research Inventory

Date: 2026-09-13  
Authority boundary: inventory and public-state-transfer aid; not a new canonical theory or evidence upgrade.

## Method

The audit searched by function, not title. An item qualified when its operation can alter sensing, retained formation, reasoning, admissibility, reachability, state transfer, developmental testing, recovery, verification, energy available to computation, or governed action. Duplicate worktrees, backups, generated copies, and imported snapshots were collapsed into one lineage entry and were not counted as independent evidence.

Source precedence was: frozen executable or receipt; explicit canon; CF v0.4 RC1; project authority; current synthesis; older round; public copy; prompt; filename inference. Local research sources were not modified.

## Access And Completeness Boundary

This is a broad functional inventory, not a forensic image of every byte on every drive. Encrypted, cloud-only, disconnected, permission-denied, deleted, unmounted, and third-party account content was not accessible. The designated R2R verbicentric source root was empty. Duplicate backup/worktree trees were sampled for lineage but not exhaustively re-read. Private correspondence, health, legal, financial, credentials, device identifiers, unrelated family projects, model caches, application folders, workstation paths, and third-party paper bodies were excluded by design.
