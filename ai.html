AI

We are investigating whether an artificial system can become computationally cheaper, more selective, more recoverable, and differently disposed to reason because of what has happened to it.

The first distinction

Capability is not formation.

A capable host may already know how to write, calculate, code, simulate, reason, and use tools. The developmental question is different: can experience leave executable consequence that changes what the same capable machine can notice, reject, trust, retrieve, test, recover, or reach next?

CAPABILITY != HOST POLICY != ACQUIRED FORMATION

Nothing here requires accepting Consequential Formation in advance. The architecture must survive matched baselines, causal intervention, ablation, recovery, and independent review.
