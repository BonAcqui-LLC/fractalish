Natural Math: Fixed Local Rules Become More Reliable Under Proportional Scale

Reliability increased across the tested proportional scales without increasing local rule complexity; normalized throughput did not improve.

The result

Key result

For this frozen mechanism and tested x1-x3 scaling regime, larger proportional systems created more opportunities for locally required conditions to coincide. Transported-substrate reuse became reliable in all 64 tested seeds at x2 and x3, while normalized work per agent-tick declined.

Failed preregistration preserved

The original transported-substrate gate remains failed

The frozen carry correction preregistered at least 48/64 transported-success seeds at x1. The executable result was 44/64, so cycle gate C6 failed and the full cycle remained failed.

  • A later 256-seed extension produced 171/256 successes, or 66.8%, with a Wilson 95% interval of about 60.8%-72.3%.
  • The four 64-seed blocks were 44/64, 45/64, 41/64, and 41/64.
  • Late work remained 256/256, supporting a conditional small-scale transport boundary rather than run-to-run noise.
  • The separate scale-test gate required at least 32/64 transported successes, so x1 passed that later scale-test gate without retroactively passing the original 48/64 carry gate.

What changed

What changed

  • World side scaled as 17*s, agents as 18*s^2, horizon as 512*s, formation lifetime as 7*s, and material feed per tick as s^2.
  • The number and duration of possible local causal histories increased with scale.
  • Total cycling and transported-reuse reliability increased across the tested range.

What stayed frozen

What did not change

  • Movement score, carry rule, trace rule, formation rule, maintenance priority, dissolution rule, work rule, local costs, relation count, and tie-break.
  • Movement remained one edge per tick.
  • Local formation, movement, maintenance, and work costs remained unchanged.
  • Cargo capacity remained three units of ordinary conserved material.
  • No global planner, global map, curiosity bonus, future-state representation, or broadcast knowledge was added.

Why it matters

Why it matters

  • A capability can be opportunity-limited rather than rule-limited.
  • Scale can change the space of admissible local causal histories even when the local rule does not change.
  • Before adding reasoning complexity to an unreliable system, test topology, lifetime, resource availability, transport, sparse encounter rate, and the number of independent causal histories.
What did not improve
  • Normalized work per agent-tick declined: about 0.01324 at x1, 0.00970 at x2, and 0.00867 at x3.
  • The evidence does not show that scale made the system uniformly more efficient.
  • The x1 transported-reuse result did not change and was not repaired.

Causal and diagnostic audit

Negative and diagnostic results

Maintenance-first ordering

Maintenance-first preserved sustained cycling but suppressed many moves that had been selected and were affordable before maintenance.

SUSTAINED CYCLE / WEAK TRANSPORT

Movement-first ordering

Reversing the order strengthened transport but destroyed the larger sustained-work cycle.

STRONGER TRANSPORT / CYCLE COLLAPSE

Rejected arbitration variants

Immediate-energy arbitration, terminal-life arbitration, and commitment reserve before formation did not preserve the desired cycle and universalize transport together.

FAILED

Interpretation

Interpretation boundary

A fixed set of local deterministic rules can preserve useful cycling across proportional increases in system size, while some higher-order capabilities become more reliable because larger systems create more opportunities for locally required conditions to coincide.

Scale changes the space of possible local causal histories even when the local rule does not change.

RULE COMPLEXITY != OPPORTUNITY COMPLEXITY.

Limitations

Limitations

  • Only scale factors x1, x2, and x3 were tested.
  • World size, population, horizon, lifetime, and input schedule scaled together, so this release does not isolate which scaling dimension caused the reliability change.
  • The result belongs to this deterministic integer-discrete mechanism and its declared conditions.
  • The local-condition audits identify necessary and diagnostic conditions, not a complete causal decomposition of every failed seed.

Not established

What is not established

  • Arbitrary-scale continuation or a universal scaling law.
  • Improved normalized efficiency.
  • Intelligence, life, consciousness, or biological reproduction.
  • A general or philosophical theory of emergence.
  • Retroactive passing of the original 44/64 preregistered gate.

Relation to current work

Canonical relation

This is a Natural Math mechanism-level experimental branch. It does not replace the Natural Math v5 frozen integer baseline and does not rewrite earlier Natural Math as Consequential Formation.

This result is being examined as a mechanism-level example for the broader Consequential Formation and Formative Field program: capability reliability may sometimes be limited by the availability of admissible local histories rather than by insufficient local reasoning complexity. This is interpretation, not evidence that artificial intelligence was demonstrated.
