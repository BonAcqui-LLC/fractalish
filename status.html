Status

Established, under review, and not claimed.

This page separates established terms, active hypotheses, non-claims, and current proof targets.

Established

Fractalish, Natural Math, MCVA, AMCVA, HOLD, recovery wake, Cognitive Basin, BasinLab naming, Activation Runtime, and the not-consciousness boundary language.

Under review

Geometry-state as native script, morphology inversion, quantified AMCVA, cross-domain comparison scoring, Fractalish AI mobile activation, CNTM realization, and the coupling between Natural Math generation and Fractalish readout.

Proof targets

Recover hidden generating parameters from synthetic morphology better than chance, compare inferred morphology families against baselines, and preserve uncertainty honestly where no stable readout exists.

What remains hypothesis

  • That process memory can be recovered robustly enough to support cross-domain comparison beyond analogy.
  • That decision points can be parameterized and compared across sufficiently different physical and cognitive regimes without collapsing important differences.
  • That Cognitive Basin logging will yield a stable morphology vocabulary rather than just interesting system telemetry.
  • That a phone-native Fractalish AI build can preserve governance and replay discipline without collapsing into a generic mobile assistant.

What is not claimed

  • No claim that consciousness has already been proven.
  • No claim of sentience.
  • No claim of artificial personhood.
  • No medical diagnostic claim.
  • No regulatory certification claim.
  • No proof of a universal law.
  • No claim that all morphology is readable.
  • No claim that fractals explain everything.
  • No claim that Natural Math models all domains.
  • No claim that AI basins prove human cognition.
  • No claim that naming Fractalish AI publicly bypasses the proof burden.

Review posture

Review is most useful when it names the exact claim, evidence state, file version, and counterexample needed to weaken or strengthen the work. Strong criticism of overclaiming is welcome. Casual dismissal is not informative.
