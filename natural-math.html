Natural Math

Natural Math is the forward or generative layer of Fractalish. It asks: if a finite system can only act locally, sense locally, spend finite energy, and remember prior constraint through its altered environment, what kinds of shapes does it tend to make?

Claim boundaryBounded modeling framework, not a universal theory of everything

What this is: a constructive, bounded framework for thinking about local updates, constraints, finite energy, branching, memory, and recovery.

What this is not: a claim that one local grammar replaces mathematics, physics, biology, or domain-specific models.

Why it exists

MCVA reads existing morphology. Natural Math generates candidate morphology. The two are complementary. One asks what process might be recoverable from already-observed form; the other asks what form finite constrained systems tend to make while moving, failing, branching, conserving, and recovering.

Core local sequence

  • EXTEND — the process continues through a locally available path.
  • SENSE — the process detects pressure, crowding, shortage, gradient, or boundary conditions.
  • RESTRICT — direct continuation becomes blocked, expensive, unstable, or locally impossible.
  • CONSERVE — the process survives by reducing activity, preserving structure, or delaying a bad move.

Dynamic limits

Natural Math assumes finite systems acting under finite constraints. That means local information bounds, finite update windows, energy accounting, and termination or inactivity conditions matter. Those limits are not annoyances to be engineered away. They are part of what shapes the morphology.

Memory becomes geometry

The next process is not born into a blank world. Prior constraints, scars, voids, deposits, repairs, callus growth, dead zones, channels, and remnant structure change what later processes encounter. In this sense, memory becomes geometry.

A later branch, crack, flow, or reasoning path does not merely inherit a history conceptually. It inherits altered terrain.

Bifurcation and recovery

Bifurcation is only part of the event. The recovery wake matters too. A local system may split, stall, reconverge, privilege one branch, or preserve a downstream scar that records the earlier obstruction. That is why Natural Math and Fractalish are both interested in semi-recovery states and altered downstream fields.

Relation to MCVA, AMCVA, and HOLD

Natural Math does not abolish the need for restraint. Even if a generative model can produce candidate morphology, AMCVA still records when the observed image is absent, obscured, erased, dominated by another geometry, or otherwise unsafe to overread. HOLD remains necessary whenever the evidence is suggestive but not yet trustworthy.

Implementation outputs

The current local implementation exports deterministic runs, event logs, history tables, and summary JSON. Over time, the important bridge is shared evidence format: generated morphology from Natural Math should be analyzable by the same Fractalish evidence package logic used for observed images.
