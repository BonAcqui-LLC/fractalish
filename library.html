Reference Library

A public registry of traces, not just pretty pictures.

The world may already have recorded the evidence. It simply did not know what it was looking at.

The library is where Fractalish stores canonical anchors, physical morphology examples, negative controls, and HOLD cases with trace files, metrics, and routing records attached.

The MVP starter set focuses on synthetic anchors and generated sample records while public-license workflows for broader physical datasets are still being stabilized.

Proof-Positive

Cases routed to MCVA with readable morphology and explicit traces.

Proof-Negative

Negative controls, AMCVA examples, and geometry-dominant counterexamples.

HOLD Cases

Likely relevant but under-supported, obscured, or domain-mismatched cases.

Canonical fractals become the alphabet. Physical morphology becomes the language.
