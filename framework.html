Framework

The grammar is upstream of the form.

Fractalish works backward from accumulated form to infer candidate operations, then forward through intervention to test whether those operations actually change what can happen next.

  1. 01

    Observe: accumulated form

    Begin with present organization as retained consequence, not as self-explaining shape.

  2. 02

    Represent: choose an instrument

    Geometry, morphology, topology, spectra, graphs, manifolds, state spaces, dynamics, and traces may all reveal structure while losing other structure.

  3. 03

    Account: preserved relation and residue

    Specify what the representation preserved, what became invisible, and what remains unobserved. Unobserved does not mean zero.

    Ageometrics · Specificity
  4. 04

    Infer: candidate grammar

    Propose formative operations only as hypotheses. GO / STOP / HOLD and PERMIT / INHIBIT / PRESERVE are not established universal primitives.

    Natural Math
  5. 05

    Predict: changed future reachability

    A formative relation matters when it changes what can happen next. The prediction must remain bounded to the declared substrate, representation, and evidence.

  6. 06

    Perturb and reconstruct

    Description reads the receipt. Intervention tests the grammar. If the form repairs, ask what maintained the machinery that maintained the form.

  7. 07

    Govern: status, lineage, and HOLD

    Public release separates VERIFIED / FROZEN, BUILT AND LOCALLY VALIDATED, SPECIFICATION, PROPOSED INTEGRATION, EXTERNAL REPORTED RESULT, negative results, and historical artifacts.

    Documents · Review

Representation boundary

A final form is lossy and often non-unique.

MCVA tests whether a chosen representation is sufficient for a target under a protocol; it is not a proof of identity or unique history. AMCVA asks what is absent, erased, or unrecoverable. HOLD is an unresolved action posture—not a substitute name for SENSE. Review posture remains explicit: SUPPORTED / UNRESOLVED / CONTRADICTED for public claims, EXTEND / HOLD / RETRACT for live Cognitive Basin handling, EXTEND / SENSE / RESTRICT for Natural Math, and GO / STOP / HOLD only as a first-verb hypothesis.
