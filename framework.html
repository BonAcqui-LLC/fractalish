Framework overview

The stack at a glance.

Fractalish is not one claim. It is a layered research stack for reading process from form, generating candidate morphologies under bounded local rules, instrumenting inward process fields, measuring representation loss, and preserving uncertainty where signal does not safely speak.

Fractalish

Process forensics: the disciplined reading of process from form. It asks whether observable morphology preserves evidence of growth, damage, recovery, constraint, erasure, pressure, routing, or memory.

Natural Math

Local transition grammar: a bounded generative framework for finite updates under energy, pressure, trail memory, conflict, and termination constraints.

Cognitive Basin

Instrumented process field: memory, contradiction scars, trust channels, recovery routes, replay, and governed recurrence over time.

Ageometrics / GSR

Representation sufficiency measurement: what a declared geometry preserves, what residue remains outside it, and which comparator controls keep the claim honest.

The Specificity Thesis

Governed representation debt and repair: receipts, residue, structural debt, action posture, replay, and later correction without erasing the earlier failure.

MCVA / AMCVA / HOLD

MCVA records where morphology may safely speak. AMCVA records where it does not safely speak. HOLD preserves uncertainty when evidence, baseline, or segmentation is insufficient.

SymLan / Construction A+ / InfinitySight

Symbolic and perception-facing lanes for stable symbolic structures, machine-native records, and future module languages earned from settled substrate structure.

CNTM / Device Realization

The physical realization path: morphology-bearing substrates, sensors, hardware evidence, and durable memory traces.

Current integration statement

Fractalish is process forensics. Cognitive Basin is the instrumented process field. Natural Math is the local transition grammar. Ageometrics and Specificity measure what representation preserves and what debt remains. MCVA is the vocabulary. HOLD is the restraint against false closure.
