Search syntax and examples
cognitive basinBoth whole words must occur. Same as cognitive AND basin.
"natural math"Words together, in that order.
MCVA OR AMCVAEither word. The pipe | also means OR.
morphology NOT quantumRequire morphology, exclude quantum. -quantum works too.
(MCVA OR AMCVA) AND "natural math"Group alternatives, then narrow them.
morph*Explicit prefix matching: morphology, morphological, and other words beginning with morph.

Write operators in UPPERCASE. Precedence: parentheses, NOT, AND, OR. Matching is case-insensitive; punctuation separates words, including within phrases. No stemming, synonyms, or automatic query expansion. Quote an operator to search for the word itself, such as "NOT".

Search covers public HTML page content, excluding repeated navigation and footers. Linked PDF, Word, ZIP, and external file contents are not indexed. Results rank by matching titles, headings, term rarity, and body text. All results must satisfy your expression.
