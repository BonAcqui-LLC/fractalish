## Pre-Edit Audit

### Current public thesis

The working public site already says: "Fractalish studies how past change becomes present structure and changes what can happen next." It presents Consequential Formation as the framework, Erase the Categories as a separate investigative method, and named projects as experiments, implementations, and evidence. It also preserves the core chain `change -> retained consequence -> altered constraint -> changed reachable futures` and generally labels candidate, earned, historical, and prohibited claims separately.

### Divergence from CF v0.3

The current public explanation is materially thinner than v0.3 in these respects:

- category suspension is presented as an optional or adjacent method rather than the constitutive first operation of CF;
- the independence audit and `TYPE THE RETURN` correction are absent from the current framework path;
- the recursive instruction to remove CF's own returned nouns is absent;
- the time / energy / matter / gravity round is summarized as noun removal but not explained as a calibration that exposed mutual constitution without identity;
- the current working types for access, timescale, closure, recoverability, and intervention family are absent;
- `CAPABILITY != FORMATION` is not a public engineering distinction;
- Minimum Formative Machine v0.1 is not visible as a SPECIFICATION / immediate BUILD-TEST TARGET;
- typed consequence writes, twin-history testing, single-write/subgraph transplant, matched baselines, and MFM kill conditions are absent;
- CNTM and biological-development material does not yet state strongly enough that biology supplies routines rather than a required implementation script;
- the machine-speed developmental-search advantage and its no-fixed-calendar-claim boundary are absent;
- the supplied v0.3 canon and compact team state-transfer packet are not yet in the public repository or document index.
