One minute before the machinery.

Fractalish studies how past changes leave structure behind that changes what can happen next. Consequential Formation is the framework now organizing that work.

One-minute CF

Things happen. Some changes leave consequences. Those consequences change what can happen next.

That is the front door. A trail, scar, habit, channel, memory, convention, trained model, worn machine, repaired organism, or inherited theory may carry consequences forward. CF asks what remained and how that remainder changes future reachability.

Five-minute CF

  1. 01

    Occurrence

    Something changes: contact, injury, learning, use, measurement, selection, construction, failure, repair.

  2. 02

    Formation

    Some consequence remains in structure, state, relation, constraint, memory, trace, or recovery route.

  3. 03

    Constraint

    The retained consequence alters what is easy, hard, possible, unavailable, costly, or recoverable.

  4. 04

    Reachability

    The future is not only a timeline. It is a changing space of reachable transitions.

  5. 05

    Test

    A CF claim should say what observation, intervention, ablation, or comparison would prove this interpretation wrong.

Why Fractalish?

The name still fits.

Fractalish began by following clues from form, morphology, fractals, retained structure, developmental pathways, and recurring patterns. CF is what emerged when those clues were pushed upstream.

The fractal was a clue. The "ish" is the investigation.

What CF does not claim

It does not claim that familiar categories are false, that resemblance proves mechanism, that history acts magically, that every object is intelligent, or that consciousness has been solved.
