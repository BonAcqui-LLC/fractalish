A practical path into the Fractalish workbench.

If you are new to Fractalish, start with the public vocabulary, then move into the current implementation surfaces, the Natural Math and CNTM lanes, the device work, and the public repositories where collaboration now happens. Documents explain the framework. Code, traces, datasets, study guides, and replay test it.

Recommended order

  1. MCVA / AMCVA / HOLD for the core vocabulary and guardrails.
  2. Natural Math for Melissa Clow's current finite local-process modeling program.
  3. Cognitive Basin / BasinLab for the governed executable architecture and replay logic.
  4. CNTM realization for the canonical-library and source-body curation lane.
  5. Motorola activation for the device evidence and phone path.
  6. Fractalish AI for the public build-program overview.
  7. Tom Wessels study guide for a public-facing example of process memory preserved in form.
  8. Recovery Wake for a concrete candidate descriptor family.
  9. Reference Library and Commons for evidence packages, examples, and comparison lanes.
  10. Documents and Working Papers for the longer specifications, notes, white papers, and implementation-facing summaries.

What to look for

Look for cases where visible form preserves enough structure to support cautious comparison, descriptor extraction, and process-memory hypotheses. Also look for the cases where the honest output is AMCVA or HOLD instead of over-reading.

How to help

The best contributions now include proof-positive cases, proof-negative cases, sharper guardrails, source reconciliation, Android and systems help, deterministic replay critique, cleaner study guides, and implementation review.
