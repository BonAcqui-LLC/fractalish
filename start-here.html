Five-minute path

Fractalish starts from a simple question: if a process grows locally, blindly, under contact and constraint, what history remains visible in the structure it leaves behind?

Natural Math studies those local updates. Fractalish studies the morphology they leave in the world. Cognitive Basin applies the same discipline inward to memory, contradiction, continuity, and action. Ageometrics asks what a final geometry keeps, what it loses, and what must be restored when compression hides the path that produced the result.

Think of a river delta, root system, or scar

The final shape is not random decoration. It carries partial evidence of pressures, routes, splits, dead ends, and recovery.

Framework Evidence records

Now apply that discipline to machine systems

Instead of disposable output alone, ask whether memory, contradiction, and repair can remain inspectable inside a governed executable system.

Technical reading path

  1. MCVA / AMCVA / HOLD for the core vocabulary and guardrails.
  2. Natural Math for the current finite local-process modeling program.
  3. Ageometrics and Geometric Sufficiency Ratio for the geometry boundary and metric layer.
  4. Ageometrics and the Geometric Sufficiency Ratio v0.5 for the full working paper and illustration package.
  5. Cognitive Basin / BasinLab for governed execution, replay, and persistent state.
  6. CNTM realization for the consolidated reference library behind the implementation route.
  7. Motorola activation for the device evidence and native systems path.
  8. Evidence records, Commons, and the Tom Wessels study guide for cross-checking examples.
  9. Documents, Review, and the public repositories when you want to inspect or challenge a specific claim.

What to watch for

Claims, evidence, restraint. The central question on every page is not whether a term sounds appealing, but whether the trace, evidence, and boundary are visible enough to survive inspection.
