Fractalish Commons

An open MCVA / AMCVA testing portal.

Fractalish Commons is not a simple fractal detector. It is an open morphology and geometry-signal workbench.

Users should be able to upload a single image, an image pair, or a sequence; let the system attempt automatic morphology-family identification; generate an SVG trace; export metrics; and test whether the signal survives cross-domain comparison.

Similarity is not identity. A fractal reading is not forced. It must be earned.

Submit a Case

Prepare a proof-positive, proof-negative, or HOLD submission with metadata.

Analyze

Run descriptor extraction, geometry scoring, AMCVA routing, and export generation.

Compare Domains

Put two traces side by side and inspect what travels and what does not.
