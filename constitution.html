Methodological commitments inside Consequential Formation.

Fractalish studies how past change becomes present structure and changes what can happen next. Consequential Formation is the current framework organizing that inquiry.

The constitutional principles below preserve methodological commitments accumulated through earlier stages of the program. In that lineage: Form is accumulated consequence. Geometry is one way of measuring the receipt. Fractalish seeks the generative grammar upstream of accumulated form.

Status: living constitutional record. These principles sit inside Consequential Formation; they do not form a competing public thesis or declare a completed theory of nature.

Constitutional Center

Fractalish studies the processes by which observable organization comes to exist, persists, changes, repairs, reconstructs, and constrains what can happen next.

Accumulated Form is an observable state whose present organization is the retained consequence of prior transitions, interactions, constraints, perturbations, selections, persistence, release, and history.

Form is evidence. Form is not automatically explanation.

The "ish"

The fractal was a clue. The "ish" is the search upstream of observable form for the relations and operations that produced it.

Representation

No representation is privileged. Geometry, topology, morphology, graphs, spectra, state spaces, phase spaces, and traces are instruments with residue.

First verb

GO / STOP / HOLD and PERMIT / INHIBIT / PRESERVE are candidate hypotheses, not established universal primitives.

Experimental recursion

Description reads the receipt. Intervention tests the grammar.

Fractalish works backward from accumulated form to infer formative operations and forward through experiment to determine whether those inferred operations actually generate, predict, arrest, preserve, repair, redirect, or reconstruct consequential organization.

The central intervention question is: what change in the conditions of persistence makes the existing form no longer self-maintaining?

  1. PossibilityMultiple futures may be accessible before a consequential transition.
  2. ConstraintOperations and interactions change what can happen next.
  3. PersistenceSome consequences remain active after the initiating event passes.
  4. PerturbationChanging the suspected relation tests whether the inferred grammar has causal value.

Evidence Discipline

Similarity is not mechanism

Visual resemblance can motivate a hypothesis, but it does not establish shared cause.

Unobserved does not mean zero

Failure of an instrument to observe something is not evidence that the thing is absent.

HOLD before false closure

Negative findings, failed experiments, counterexamples, and unresolved questions remain part of lineage.

Desiloization

Decompartmentalization without decontextualization. Disciplines are metadata, not walls. Cross-domain abstraction must never overwrite domain-native terminology, mechanism, scale, uncertainty, provenance, or failure modes.

Claim Boundary

This Constitution does not claim a universal grammar of nature, proof that nature is fundamentally fractal, proof that GO / STOP / HOLD are universal primitives, proof of machine consciousness, proof of artificial life, equivalence between biological and artificial systems based on resemblance, causal inference from morphology alone, semantic truth from traces, a universal decoder, or completion of the Fractalish research program.
